Windows

Windows 10 or 11. No Python, no installer.

  1. Download MudaRemote.exe from the latest release.
  2. Put it in its own folder. It keeps its settings next to itself. The desktop is fine.
  3. Open it. Quick Setup asks for your Discord token, the channel, what kind of rolls you want, what to claim and which kakera to collect.
  4. Save and start. Check the summary and hit Save & Start Bot.

SmartScreen will probably complain because the build isn't code-signed yet. More info, then Run anyway. The app checks for updates when it starts.

Android

Android 8.0 or newer.

  1. Download Mudaremote.apk from the releases page. It isn't attached to every release, so scroll until you find it.
  2. Allow the install when Android asks.
  3. Import your presets from the desktop app, or set them up there.
  4. Keep the notification on and exempt the app from battery optimization, or your phone will kill it.

What you get

Everything the bot does

Rolls, wishlist and series claims, kakera with power tracking, $dk, $rt, $us, $mk, schedules, quiet hours, multiple presets.

A real settings app

Most settings live in the desktop editor. You don't have to touch a JSON file to get going.

Your data stays with you

The token and presets live on your machine. Apart from Discord, the app only talks to GitHub, to check for updates.

The catch

It only runs while your PC or phone is on and connected. That's the part Pro solves.

Before you run it

Read this before you start. MudaRemote is a selfbot. Selfbots break Discord's Terms of Service and accounts do get banned for it. The delays, quiet hours and channel-activity checks reduce ban risk. They don't remove it. Use an account you can afford to lose.

Why is it free?

Because it started as an open source project and it's staying one. If it saves you time, sponsoring keeps the fixes coming. A star, a good bug report or helping someone in the Discord helps too.
