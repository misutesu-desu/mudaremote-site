What it does

The repetitive part of Mudae, handled.

You set the rules once in a normal desktop app. No JSON editing, no Python install.

Rolls on time

Rolls the moment your rolls reset, follows the timing you pick and stays quiet during the hours you set.

Claims by your rules

Wishlist characters, series you follow, or anything over the kakera value you set. Nothing else.

Kakera, the useful ones

Reacts to the kakera colors you choose and keeps track of your power so it doesn't burn it on junk.

$dk, $rt, $us, $mk

Daily kakera, reset claim timer and the related flows run when they make sense, not when you remember.

Several accounts

Run multiple presets at once and let them coordinate instead of fighting over the same roll.

Reads the room

Can hold off while people are actively talking in the channel. Less obvious, less annoying.

The honest part

Read this before you start. MudaRemote is a selfbot. Selfbots break Discord's Terms of Service and accounts do get banned for it. The delays, quiet hours and channel-activity checks reduce ban risk. They don't remove it. Use an account you can afford to lose.

Why cloud

The free version works. Pro means your PC doesn't have to.

Same open source bot, hosted on a server. You check on it from the same app, from anywhere, while your computer is off.

  • Runs while your PC is off. No more leaving a gaming rig on all night for a Discord game.
  • Activity feed in plain words. What it claimed, what it missed, kakera collected, roll rounds, resets.
  • AI preset assistant. Say what you want in normal words. It shows the exact changes and applies nothing until you approve.
  • Recovers by itself. If Discord drops the connection or the bot crashes, it reconnects or restarts without you.
  • Local still works. Local and Cloud sit side by side in one app. Running locally is unchanged.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Will I get banned?

You might. MudaRemote is a selfbot and Discord bans selfbots. The human-ish timing, quiet hours and channel checks reduce ban risk, but no tool can promise you won't get caught. Anyone who says their selfbot is undetectable is selling you something.

Don't run it on an account you'd be upset to lose.

Is the free version actually free?

Yes. It's MIT licensed, it isn't a trial and features aren't locked behind Pro. Pro only adds hosting and the cloud extras on top.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Where the bot runs. Free runs on your PC or Android phone, so it stops when they do. Pro runs the same bot on a server and adds the activity feed, the AI preset assistant and automatic recovery.

Do I need Python or a terminal?

No. On Windows you download one .exe and open it. Quick Setup asks for your token, the channel and what you want claimed. There's also an Android app for Android 8.0 and newer.

Windows says the app might be dangerous.

That's SmartScreen. The builds aren't code-signed yet, so Windows doesn't recognize them. Click More info, then Run anyway. If you'd rather not trust a binary, the source is on GitHub and you can build it yourself.

Does Pro need my Discord token?

Yes. A selfbot logs in as you, so there's no way around it. The token is stored encrypted on the server and it's never shown to the AI assistant. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I get Pro?

Sponsor the tier you want on GitHub Sponsors, then ask for access in #pro-request on the Discord server with your GitHub name. You get a login and a key. During the closed beta, access is handed out by hand.

Mudae changed something and the bot broke.

It happens, Mudae and Discord change things. Report it in the Discord or open a GitHub issue. Fixes usually ship as a new release and the app tells you when one is out.

Try the free one first.

If it does what you need, keep using it. If you get tired of leaving your PC on, Pro is there.
