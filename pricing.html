How paying works

There's no checkout on this site. Payment runs through GitHub Sponsors and access is handed out in Discord.

  1. Sponsor the tier. Pick it on GitHub Sponsors. Monthly or one-time, depending on the tier.
  2. Ask for access. Post in #pro-request on the Discord server with your GitHub name.
  3. Sign in. You get a login and a key. Put the key in the Account tab of the app.

Refunds

If Pro doesn't work for you, ask within 7 days of a payment and you get that payment back. Founder gets 14 days. A Discord ban isn't grounds for a refund, the risk is spelled out up front. Full refund policy.

Pro is in closed beta. Expect rough edges and tell me about them in #pro-feedback.
