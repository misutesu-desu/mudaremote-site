Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

What each part of MudaRemote collects, where it goes and how to get it deleted.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no forms.
  • It's hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Some pages ask the GitHub API for the date of the latest release, so GitHub sees that request.
  • If you switch the theme, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.

The free app

  • It runs on your device. Your Discord token and presets stay there.
  • Apart from Discord, it only talks to GitHub, to check for and download updates.
  • I don't receive anything from the free app.

MudaRemote Pro

To run your bot on the server, Pro stores:

  • Your Pro login and key.
  • Your Discord token, encrypted at rest. The bot needs it to log in as you.
  • Your presets.
  • The bot's activity history: rolls, claims, kakera, resets and errors, so the app can show you the feed.

The AI preset assistant sends your messages and your current preset settings to a third-party AI model provider to generate the suggested changes. Your Discord token is never sent to it or shown to it.

I don't sell any of this or use it for ads. I only look at your data when you ask for help or to fix a problem with your bot.

Payments

Payment goes through GitHub Sponsors. I see your GitHub username and your tier. GitHub handles the card details and I never see them.

Discord

If you join the Discord server, Discord's own privacy policy applies to what you post there.

Deleting your data

Ask in the Discord server and I'll delete your Pro account, stored token, presets and activity history. You can also change your Discord password at any time, which invalidates the stored token.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and important changes get announced in the Discord server.
